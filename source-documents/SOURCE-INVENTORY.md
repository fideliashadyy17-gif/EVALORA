### Why this is Standard, not Extended

Brilliant Learning publishes the two levels on separate pages and under separate
filenames:

- Standard, May 2025 → `M25-Maths-MYP.pdf` (page `2025-standard-math-past-papers/`)
- Extended, May 2025 → `M25-Extended-Maths.pdf` (page `2025-extended-maths-past-papers/`)

The file transcribed here is the one linked from the **Standard** page. It is also
consistent with Standard content: indices, fractions, surds, simultaneous
equations, sample-space probability, cumulative frequency, quadratic rectangle
model, sector/triangle mensuration, and coordinate geometry. There are no
logarithms, no sine/cosine rules, no sequences, no matrices and no networks —
the topics that the Extended framework adds. It is not a DP paper (no calculus,
no distributions, no vectors).

### NOT recovered from the paper

The diagrams are images inside the PDF and were not rendered into the extracted
text. The paper states their dimensions, which were recovered:

- Q5 rectangle: one side `5x`, area `140 units²`
- Q6 Design T: isosceles triangle, `h` to be shown as 3022 m to the nearest metre
- Q6 Design S: sector QRS, `RQ = RS = 3400 m`
- Q7 warehouse: rectangle 32 m by 16 m, A(0,0), P(16,12), B(32,16)
- Q4: cumulative-frequency graph, whose underlying data tables **were** recovered

The sandbox has no outbound network access except to github.com / npm / pypi, so
the PDF cannot be downloaded into the workspace and its embedded images cannot be
extracted.

## 4. Examination settings — OBTAINED (secondary sources, not the paper itself)

The extracted question paper text did not contain the front-matter instructions.
These values come from published descriptions of the MYP Mathematics eAssessment
and are consistent across several independent sources:

| Setting | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 2 hours (120 minutes) | revisiondojo.com "How Long Are MYP eAssessment Exams?"; edflik.com; vidyalai.com |
| Total marks | 100 | "MYP E-Assessments May 2025 – Maths Revision Guide": *"Exam length: 2 hours 100 Marks"* |
| Criterion split | A ≈ 33–34, B ≈ 33–34, D ≈ 33–34; C assessed throughout | same revision guide |
| Calculator | On-screen calculator provided by the platform; personal handheld calculator not normally permitted | revisiondojo.com "Materials Allowed in MYP Extended Mathematics eAssessment" |
| Permitted resources | Only resources embedded in the examination. No personal formula sheet, textbook or notes | same |
| Formula booklet | Provided **on-screen inside** the examination; students navigate it digitally | edflik.com; getyourtutors.com |

**Caveat:** these were not read off the May 2025 paper's own instructions page,
because that page did not survive the text extraction. They are documented IB
eAssessment conventions, not verified from the specific paper.
