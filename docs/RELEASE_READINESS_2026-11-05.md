## Production database changes and tests

- Migration 0011 (`class_question_bank_events`) was already applied earlier; do **not** replay it. Production received five migrations on 5 November: `atomic_class_associations` (local 0023), `protect_assessment_content` (0024), `protect_member_associations` (0025), `protect_roster_and_exam_data` (0026) and `protect_access_and_imports` (0027). All were recorded in Supabase migration history. They do not modify existing rows; 0023 adds a restricted transaction function and 0024–0027 change policies.
- 0023 atomically validates and replaces class subject/programme associations. Production schema/grants and function execution privileges were checked; only `service_role` can execute it. In a live transaction rolled back at the end, two synthetic organizations were used to verify successful association creation and rejection of cross-tenant selections. A subsequent query found no remaining synthetic organizations. An isolated PostgreSQL-compatible PGlite fixture checked rollback after an injected write failure. Class detail changes still precede the RPC in a separate transaction; failure is explicitly reported as partial success.
- A live read-only simulated role check found that an existing DEMO student could read correct options. Existing permissive `FOR ALL` policies also allowed organization members to delete bank/paper or association records because `USING` applies to DELETE. 0024 restricts question/paper reads to staff while retaining sanitized participant APIs, and restricts bank/paper writes to creators or Super Admin. 0025 preserves member association reads but restricts writes to creators or Super Admin. No customer records were deleted.
- After 0024, actual RLS checks using DEMO identities verified that a DEMO student could not see questions, options or paper links; a DEMO teacher could read its own question bank but not other tenants; and `service_role` retained access. A random, unassociated authenticated identity saw no exams, attempts, answers or questions. These are scoped checks, not a full penetration test.
- A DEMO student was also found to be able to read other students’ roster records. 0026 restricts roster and enrollment reads to the person or staff and restricts exam details to staff. 0027 restricts competition access codes and import records to staff, and registration records to their owner or staff. Post-migration actual DEMO role simulations confirmed student restrictions and teacher roster access; isolated fixtures checked staff, Super Admin and service role behavior. No full authenticated browser E2E was performed.
- After 0025, a production rollback-only synthetic fixture added a separate temporary organization, class, subject, programme, event and phase, with DEMO test identities scoped to that temporary organization. A student retained reads but could not delete any of the four fixture association types; a teacher could delete fixture rows. The entire fixture was rolled back. Isolated PGlite tests also exercise students, staff, Super Admin, service role and cross-tenant access. No authenticated browser or LiveKit end-to-end acceptance is implied.
- Before migration, DB/Auth/REST reported healthy. Read-only aggregates found 15 examinations with nonempty same-organization papers, 1 attempt (none in progress), 8 answer associations without mismatch and 1,048 quiz-question links without cross-organization links or negative points. Counts are time-specific and do not prove concurrency safety.

## Remaining limitations

Exam saves, submission and marking still consist of separate database writes and are not race-safe or exactly-once; multi-tab/deadline and live database failure injection remain unverified. Editing question options can change or remove IDs used by historical attempts. Public media bucket policies, token exposure/rotation, LiveKit operation, other RLS policies (including billing visibility), entitlements, rate limits and full hostile-tenant behavior require further assessment. Vercel preview and production environments share the production Supabase project and service role; previews are not an isolated test environment and must not be used for destructive tests. Corrector/supervisor behavior was tested in an isolated fixture, not with production role accounts. Secure-exam lockdown, AI Studio, paper-mode grading and integrations are not delivered end-to-end. Do not represent these checks as completion of the roadmap.
